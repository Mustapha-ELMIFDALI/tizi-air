Your day,
start to finish

About half a day from your door and back again. Scroll to follow the route.

To Marrakech · 45 min Take-off Azlag · tea Landing N
  1. 1

    Pickup in Marrakech

    We meet you at your riad or hotel in the morning or the afternoon, depending on the flight you booked.

  2. 2

    Drive to Aguergour

    Forty-five minutes out of the city, heading south toward the foothills of the Atlas.

  3. 3

    Mint tea in Azlag

    A short stop in the village of Azlag for a glass of tea while the pilots check the wind.

  4. 4

    Up to take-off

    The track climbs onto the high ground east of Aguergour. Your pilot fits your helmet and harness and walks you through take-off and landing.

  5. 5

    Take-off

    The wing fills, you take a few running steps together, and your feet leave the ground.

  6. 6

    The flight

    Ride the lift in front of the ridge, then glide west over the valleys and Aguergour with the Atlas behind you. Ask for gentle turns or a few spirals.

  7. 7

    Landing

    A slow final turn and a soft landing in the fields beside the Marrakech road, usually on your feet.

  8. 8

    Back to the city

    The car is waiting at the landing field to drive you back to your door in Marrakech.

Questions,
answered

Anything else, ask in the notes when you book and we'll reply.

Do I need any experience?
None at all. On a tandem flight the pilot does the flying; you sit in front in a comfortable harness and enjoy the view. All you need to do is run a few steps at take-off.
What should I wear?
Closed shoes with grip, long trousers and a warm layer. It's cooler and windier on the ridge than in Marrakech, especially in the morning and at sunset. Sunglasses help.
Can I get photos and video of my flight?
Yes. Pilots fly with a camera on a pole and can film the whole flight. Mention it when you book and we'll confirm the details.
What if the weather is bad?
Safety decides. If the wind or weather isn't right on the day, we'll move your flight to another slot. If no other date works for you, we'll refund you.
Is there an age or weight limit?
Children and older guests fly with us regularly. Tell us the age and weight of each passenger in your booking request and we'll confirm before your flight.
How do I get to the ridge?
The Aguergour ridge is 45 minutes from Marrakech. We pick you up from your riad or hotel and drive you back after the flight.
